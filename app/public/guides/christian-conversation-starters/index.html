Spill All starters

Christian conversation starters for real faith

A good Christian conversation starter does not perform spirituality. It makes it safer to say what is actually true.

Spill started as a simple product: pick your packs, choose a depth, and draw a card. The questions themselves used to live behind a tap. This page is the public version of that deck.

Use a light starter to open the room. Use an honest question when people are already present. Save the deep cards for trust. If you want the next card without scrolling a list, draw from the full library.

Questions people ask

What makes a conversation starter Christian?

Not every card names Jesus out loud. The Christian ones are written from a worldview of honesty before God, repair, prayer, scripture, and shared life in the church. Spill also lets you filter for more overt language.

How do I use these with people who are not in church?

Start light. Ask one question, then stay quiet long enough for a real answer. You do not need to turn the moment into a lesson.

Is this a printable card game?

Spill is a web deck you can open on a phone in seconds. Share a card link if you want one question on everyone's screen.
